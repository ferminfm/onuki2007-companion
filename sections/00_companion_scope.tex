\section{Companion Scope and Reading Policy}
\label{sec:companion-scope}

\sourceeqrange{whole paper; navigation only}

\subsection*{Purpose}

This companion is an original derivation guide for \OnukiPRE. The source
paper remains the authority for the published model, figures, and narrative.
The companion cites equation numbers and records assumptions. It reconstructs the calculations independently while preserving the source context.

\subsection*{Source-equation badges}

Displayed equations carrying a small badge such as \sourceeqbadge{2.35}
are direct reconstructions or notation-normalized forms of the cited Onuki
source equation.  A badge beginning with ``Companion'' instead marks a
companion-derived expression that is compared with a printed source equation
but is not itself the printed formula.  Unbadged displayed
equations are companion derivation steps, product-rule expansions, type checks,
or diagnostic residuals unless the surrounding prose explicitly says otherwise.

\subsection*{Companion code and verification appendix}

The directory \texttt{scripts/} contains finite symbolic and dimensional
checks linked to the derivations in this paper, and \texttt{tests/} contains
regression checks for ledgers and source-boundary claims.
Appendix~\ref{app:verification-plan} summarizes what those checks cover and,
just as importantly, what they do not prove.

\subsection*{Reproducibility complement}

The tag-pinned verification complement is available at (version
\OnukiVerificationTag):
\begin{center}
\small \OnukiVerificationRepository
\end{center}
It provides hand routes and listed finite checks for each topic; it does not
establish a continuum PDE, a constitutive law, simulation fidelity, or full
model equivalence. \OnukiLinkVerificationIndex{The verification index} is the
entry point, and \OnukiLinkFormalScope{the finite formal-scope note} states the
Lean boundary.

\subsection*{What the companion provides}

\begin{itemize}
\item Source-equation navigation for the main thermodynamic, variational,
  hydrodynamic, boundary, and scaled-equation formulas.
\item Independent derivation steps for the identities currently covered.
\item Explicit separation of local, equilibrium, boundary, global, and
  simulation-specialized assumptions.
\item Verification checks for finite algebraic and dimensional claims.
\end{itemize}
