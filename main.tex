\begin{abstract}
This is an original guided companion for studying Onuki's 2007 dynamic van der
Waals paper. It follows the source section and equation order for navigation,
while keeping the original paper as the source authority. This version derives
the Section II.A equilibrium baseline, Sections II.B through II.D local
identities, Appendix A reversible stress bookkeeping, and Appendix B scaled
equations, and it provides a source-bounded Section III simulation reading guide
without numerical reproduction. Appendix E reconstructs a separate,
conditional local-stress crosswalk from an inspected Korteweg 1901 source; it
does not supply Onuki's entropy, energy, wall, or full PDE model.
\end{abstract}
