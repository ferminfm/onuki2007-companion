\label{app:crosswalk-open-issues}

\sourceeqrange{crosswalk across Eqs. (2.1)--(B6) and Section III estimates}

\subsection*{Purpose}

This appendix collects notation and source-dependent issues that should not
interrupt the section-by-section reading.  It also records which open issues are
mathematical branch questions and which are numerical-code questions.

\subsection*{Crosswalk Entries}

\begin{itemize}
\item \(n\): number density.  Mass density appears only after multiplying by a
  molecular mass.
\item \(T\): local temperature; \(\Theta=k_BT/\epsilon\) is its Appendix-B scaled
  form.
\item \(C,K,M\): gradient coefficients with \(M=CT+K\); Section III uses the
  special branch \(K=0\) and constant \(C\).
\item \(\hat\mu\): generalized chemical potential derived from the gradient
  entropy/energy functional.
\item \(\Pi_{ij}\): pressure and stress tensor.  Its Appendix-B diagonal
  branch is tracked in printed and dimensional-source forms.
\item \(p_1\): diagonal gradient-pressure scalar, with \(M_n|_T\) distinguished
  from a spatial derivative of \(M\).
\item \(J_s^{\rm rev}\) and \(J_{\rm GA}\): local reversible entropy-flux and
  alternative capillary energy-flux objects; their local gauge relation is not a
  global balance by itself.
\item \(S_s,E_s\): surface entropy and surface energy objects that enter global
  wall/boundary transfer.
\item \(\phi=v_0n\), \(V=t_0v/\ell\), \(E_T=e_Tv_0/\epsilon\), \(\sigma\), and
  \(g^*\): Appendix-B and Section III scaled variables and estimate parameters.
\end{itemize}
