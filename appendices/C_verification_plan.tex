\section{Verification Plan}
\label{app:verification-plan}

\sourceeqrange{finite identities only}

\subsection*{Purpose}

This appendix describes the verification layer. It is not evidence that the PDE
model, constitutive laws, simulations, or physical conclusions are proved by
software.  The verification scripts check algebraic, dimensional, and ledger
claims that are finite enough to be independently reconstructed.

\subsection*{Tool Layers}

\begin{itemize}
\item Wolfram: primary symbolic residuals for finite tensor/component identities.
\item SymPy: open-source mirrors for fragile symbolic rows.
\item Pint: dimensional consistency for scales, fluxes, and estimate variables.
\item pytest: ledger, source-scope, and regression checks.
\item Lean/Mathlib: finite algebraic and logical kernels for the inspected
  Korteweg tensor; these kernels do not differentiate fields or prove a
  continuum model.  Maxima remains available for later scalar third-witness
  checks if needed.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Central Runner}

The command
\[
\texttt{bash scripts/run\_verification.sh}
\]
runs the companion integrity checks, all implemented SymPy/Pint scripts, all
pytest regression files, and the available Wolfram scripts.  Generated summaries
are written under \texttt{generated/} and remain untracked.

For a script-by-script map from code to the companion text, see the CSV file
\begin{center}
\small\texttt{research\_notes/}\\
\texttt{onuki\_companion\_code\_to\_paper\_map.csv}.
\end{center}
This public verification index records script paths, tool layers, companion locations,
source-equation anchors, checked identities, generated summary paths when
available, and proof-scope limits.  It is a reader-facing index for the
verification code; the scripts themselves keep the full executable details.
Two specialized row indexes are
\path{research_notes/visible_derivation_code_to_equation_map.csv} and
\path{research_notes/source_fidelity_code_to_equation_map.csv}.  They record,
respectively, the public derivation links and the highest-risk source-fidelity
checks without changing the proof scope of the scripts.
The exhaustive coauthor-review join is
\path{research_notes/coauthor_readiness_code_to_paper_map.csv}.  It gives every
public relation an explicit finite-evidence status or an explicit
nonalgebraic/contextual not-applicable status.  It does not convert grouped
residual checks into proofs of continuum statements.  Its check identifiers
name either a direct relation-specific match or the exact enclosing displayed
derivation step, and its separate source-fidelity columns preserve bounded
source discrepancies even when the finite algebraic check passes.

\subsection*{Current Implemented Checks}

\begin{itemize}
\item Section II.A: local thermodynamic identities, coexistence conditions,
  interface first integral, surface-tension density, and fragile-term negative
  controls.
\item Section II.B: coefficient identities, the temperature-correction identity,
  the diagnostic term-removal test expression, and a one-dimensional boundary
  integration-by-parts identity.
\item Section II.C: equilibrium integration by parts, natural wall-boundary
  cancellation, quadratic wall derivative, planar surface-tension check, and
  expected nonzero omitted-wall residual.
\item Appendix A: virtual-displacement density variation, product-rule
  expansion, one-dimensional gradient integration by parts, stress-power
  component contraction, off-diagonal stress symmetry, and expected nonzero
  omitted-off-diagonal residual.
\item Section II.D: kinetic-energy bookkeeping, internal-energy balance as total
  energy minus kinetic energy, stress-flux product rule, simplified viscous
  production proxy, local entropy-flux gauge relation, derivative-expanded
  \(p_1\), expected nonzero omitted-reversible-flux and derivative-misuse
  residuals, and a boundary-transfer regression.
\item Appendix B: scale definitions, B2 momentum scaling, branchwise B3 pressure
  tensor residual, B4 energy density, B5 local total-energy equation, B6 density
  boundary condition, and expected nonzero omitted wall/global-transfer
  residual.
\item Section III: finite reading-guide estimates only: \(\sigma\), \(g^*\), the
  sound scale, latent-heat velocity units, effective-conductivity and Nusselt
  definitions, the adiabatic temperature-gradient units, and a regression that
  simulations are not reproduced.
\item Korteweg 1901: component reconstruction of Eq.~(20), its local divergence,
  state-coefficient chain rules, pressure-to-Cauchy sign conversion, dimensions,
  and negative controls for omitted coefficient derivatives and unsupported
  scalar gauges.  A second finite suite checks the conditional Korteweg--Onuki
  local-stress map and its general nonisothermal residual.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{What Is Not Verified}

The checks do not prove continuum PDE equivalence, the second law,
well-posedness, numerical convergence, empirical validity, published figure
values, or the unpublished simulation-code branch.  Local flux identities remain
local unless boundary and wall-transfer terms are supplied.
